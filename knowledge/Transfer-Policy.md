# Harbor & Pine Foods: Inventory Transfer Policy

_Version 1.0 · October 2026 · Owner: Inventory Coordinator · All data fictitious._

## 1. Purpose
Staff move stock between locations to fix shortages. This policy makes sure a transfer that helps one location does not leave another one short, overstocked, or holding food that will spoil.

## 2. Who does what
- **Requester:** any staff member. Submits the request: item, quantity, from location, to location, need-by date.
- **Approver:** the manager of the **receiving** location. A person always makes the approval decision.
- **Nobody approves their own request.** The assistant never approves, rejects, or changes a decision.
- **Inventory Coordinator:** maintains locations, items, stock levels and this policy.

## 5. The four screening checks
Every submitted request is checked automatically against these fixed rules before a manager sees it.

1. **Excess quantity:** the quantity is more than 5 times the item's standard reorder quantity. Example: Olive Oil reorder is 15, so more than 75 cases is excess.
2. **Source safety stock:** the source location's on-hand quantity minus the transfer quantity falls below the source's safety stock.
3. **Destination overstock:** after the transfer, the destination would hold more than 21 days of supply. Days of supply = (destination on hand + quantity) divided by the destination's average daily usage.
4. **Perishable spoilage:** the item is perishable and the destination's days of supply after the transfer is longer than the item's shelf life.

A request to a location that has **no stock record** for the item is flagged for review: confirm the location carries the item before approving.

## 6. Risk levels
- **Clear:** no checks triggered. The manager can approve normally.
- **Review:** one check triggered, or a stock record is missing. The manager reads the screening notes before deciding.
- **Block:** excess quantity plus at least one other check. The manager may approve only with a written override reason in the approval comments.

The risk level is set by the rules, never by the assistant or the requester.

## 7. Timing
- Requests submitted by 2:00 PM are reviewed the same business day.
- Need-by dates must be at least one business day after submission.
- Urgent requests: mark the priority High and add a note explaining why.

## 8. What happens after a decision
The requester receives an email with the decision, the risk level, the screening notes and the approver's name. Approved transfers move to In Transit, then Received when they arrive.
